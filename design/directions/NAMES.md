# Product name options

**Status (2026-09-24):** Barton approved Direction A and is keeping **Mintmark** as the working name. Options 4 to 10 were added as further alternatives.

"Hawk.fun" was the placeholder in the old prototype. Three alternatives follow. **None has been checked for domain or trademark availability yet.** Run domain, trademark (USPTO/EUIPO) and social-handle searches before committing to any of them.

1. **Mintmark** (recommended). A mint mark is the small stamp on a coin that shows where it was struck. That links coins (tokens) to minting (NFTs) in one short, calm, premium word that sounds like a real brand rather than a meme. *Availability not checked. It is also a common numismatic term, which may make a trademark harder to secure; check early.*
2. **Obverse**. The obverse is the face side of a coin. It hints that tokens and NFTs are two sides of the same asset, and it has a gallery/numismatic elegance that fits Direction B especially well. *Availability not checked.*
3. **Recast**. It means melting tokens into an NFT and back, and recasting memes as culture. It works as a verb in the product ("recast 1,000,000 NOCT into an NFT") and has a modern fintech ring. *Availability not checked.*

All three mockup directions use **Mintmark**, marked as a "working name" in the nav. It is still a placeholder until the checks above are done.

## More alternatives (added 2026-09-24)

4. **Planchet**: the blank metal disc before it is struck into a coin. Tokens are the blanks and the NFT is what gets struck. Rare, distinctive and ownable.
5. **Specie**: money in coin form. Paying "in specie" means paying in the actual object rather than its cash value, which fits converting tokens into the real piece and back.
6. **Obol**: a small ancient Greek coin. Four letters and classical, with a culture-first feel. *Heads-up: a crypto project already trades as "Obol" (Obol Network, Ethereum distributed validators), so brand-conflict risk is high.*
7. **Assay**: testing a coin's metal for purity. It suggests verification and transparency, which fits the "what nobody can change" guarantees.
8. **Ingot**: a cast bar of metal. Tokens are melted down and cast into one solid piece. Short and weighty.
9. **Proofmark**: "proof" coins are the finest collector strikes, and "proof" also suggests verifiable. *Note: "proof mark" is also a firearms-testing term.*
10. **Minthouse**: the place where coins are struck; a house where collections are minted. Warm and brand-like, and a sibling to Mintmark.
