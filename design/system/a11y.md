## 2. Focus
- A global static `:focus-visible` outline: 2px `color.accent.text` (#ff9a62), offset 2px, on links, buttons, inputs, tabs and the wizard type/ratio buttons. No transition and no motion.
- A skip link ("Skip to content") is the first focusable element and jumps to `<main id="main">`.

## 3. Labels and names
- Every input, textarea and select has a label: either a `<label for>` tied to its id (the generator matches labels to inputs) or an `aria-label` (tier inputs, search). The checker found **0 unlabeled inputs**.
- Icon-only controls have names: nav search is a `<button aria-label>`, plus the mobile menu button and the chart timeframe tabs. The checker found **0 unnamed buttons**.
- The nav is `aria-label="Main"`. The wizard's type and ratio groups are `role="group"` with labels.

## 4. Images, icons and placeholders
- Decorative inline SVGs (icons beside text) get `aria-hidden="true"`. Meaningful ones are `role="img"` with a label, e.g. the price chart: "Price chart, example data". The checker found **0 unlabeled SVGs** on any page.
- The mascot boxes now show the **temporary knight placeholder** (`design/placeholder/knight-placeholder.svg`) as an `<img>` with the alt text "Placeholder art, mascot coming soon". It sits in a `<figure>` with a visible "Placeholder art" caption on boxes 120px or taller. The checker found **0 images without alt**.
- Art tiles that stand in for NFT images are labelled where they carry meaning, otherwise hidden.

## 5. Headings and landmarks
- Exactly **one h1 per page**, and no skipped levels on any page (checker: `skips=[]` everywhere).
- Fixed: token pages and portfolio jumped h1→h3 because card titles were h3. Card titles are now h2 there, and inner titles h3. Footer column titles are h2 (visually unchanged). Wizard checklist titles went from h4 to h3.
- Each page has `<nav>`, a single `<main>` and `<footer>`.

## 6. Keyboard
- Wizard type and ratio choices and the chart and tab controls are real `<button>`s. Links without a destination got `href="#"`, and disabled ones get `tabindex="-1"` with `aria-disabled`. The checker found **0 href-less anchors**.

## 7. Motion
- None. `motion.duration` = 0. There are no transitions or animations, including on focus and hover.

## Results by page (a11y_check.py, final run)
All 16 Obsidian pages and components.html: h1=1, skips=[], unlabeled=[], svg_unlabeled=0, a_nohref=0, main=1, img_noalt=0, mascot boxes labelled (all), btn_noname=[].

## Known gaps (mockup limits, not fixed)
- The mobile menu button doesn't open anything in the static mockups. The real build needs a disclosure menu with `aria-expanded`.
- Wide tables (positions, comparison, fees, activity) scroll sideways inside their card on mobile. Usable, but the real build could switch them to stacked rows.
- On mobile, the token page's trade panel comes after the chart and activity (single-column order). Consider moving it up or adding a sticky Buy bar in the real build.
- No live-region announcements for trade or convert results. That's a real-build concern because the mockups have no state changes.
- Not tested with screen readers, at 200% zoom, or in forced-colors mode.
