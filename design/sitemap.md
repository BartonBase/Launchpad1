## How Coming soon types appear
- **Tax split** and **Raffle** appear wherever all types are listed: the home comparison table, the Explore type filter, Explore's Coming soon cards and the wizard's type step.
  - They are always rendered **dim**, with a dashed outline and a "Coming soon" chip (Explore filter tabs use a "Soon" tag).
  - They can't be clicked or selected and have no token page.
  - The copy says they can't be launched yet. Comparison cells that aren't final read "Details at launch".
- When either type goes live:
  - its filter tab, picker card and table column switch to the live style;
  - a new column is added to the matrices above (expected panels: tax rate and harvest/buy-back, or round pot and draw);
  - it gets its own token page variant.
